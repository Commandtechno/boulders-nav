How it works

Routes follow the resort's roads and footpaths, like walking directions. The map turns to face the way you're walking, and voice prompts call out each turn. Hold the phone flat for a steadier compass.

Room positions come from the resort map matched to real-world coordinates and are usually within 10–20 m. At your door, tap Pin this room here once so “Take me home” ends exactly at your room.

Shuttle: text 480‑725‑7054 or dial ext. 1143.

About

Room positions and the walking network were extracted from the Boulders Resort property map and georeferenced against OpenStreetMap roads and surveyed landmarks. Compass accuracy depends on your phone: if the map spins or the arrow drifts, wave the phone in a figure‑eight.
